Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy describes how RumboRide Technologies LLC handles information in connection with this corporate website at rumboride.com.

Information you choose to provide

If you email admin@rumboride.com, you provide your email address and any information included in your message. We use that information to respond to your inquiry and communicate with you about your request. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information in initial inquiries.

Website data and hosting

This website is a static site. It does not include account registration, a contact submission form, advertising trackers, or analytics scripts. The site’s own code does not set cookies or store visitor information in browser storage.

The hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to serve and protect the website. That processing is subject to the provider’s policies.

How information is handled

Inquiry information may be processed by email and hosting service providers as needed to operate the website and respond to communications. We do not sell information provided through website inquiries. We may disclose information when required by applicable law.

Retention and security

We retain inquiry correspondence as needed to address the inquiry and meet applicable business or legal obligations. No method of electronic communication or storage can guarantee absolute security.

Questions and requests

To ask about information you have provided, or request its correction or deletion, contact admin@rumboride.com. We will consider requests in light of applicable requirements and any obligations to retain information.

Product policies and external links

This policy applies to the corporate website. HivePlant, RumboRide, and any other products may have separate privacy policies that describe their own data practices. Links to external services are governed by those services’ policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as website practices change. The date shown on this page identifies the latest revision.
